About SafeRoute DC

SafeRoute DC is a Washington, DC route-planning application. It shows walking directions and a 0-100 score calculated from historical crime records near each route.

Read scores as context, not a guarantee. The model summarizes past reported incidents. It cannot predict what will happen on a trip or account for every condition on the street.

At a glance

  • Frontend: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in public/, with Google Maps in the browser.
  • Backend: Node.js and Express, including the POST /api/safetyScore endpoint.
  • Database: MySQL, accessed through mysql2.
  • Crime data: Washington, DC MPD crime layers from the DC GIS ArcGIS Feature Service.
  • Deployment: Railway with a Docker-based service, as configured for this project.
