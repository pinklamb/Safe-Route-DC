01 / OVERVIEW

What is SafeRoute DC?

SafeRoute DC is a route-planning prototype that pairs walking directions with a score based on nearby reported crime. It is intended to help people explore route information in Washington, DC.

A score is a signal, not a guarantee.
Crime reports are historical, incomplete records of reported incidents. A route score cannot predict what will happen on a trip or replace your own judgment.

Technical notes

Distance is measured with the Haversine formula (Earth radius 6,371 km). Duplicate records are de-duplicated using latitude, longitude, and crime type. Route geometry is currently sampled at its supplied points, rather than calculating point-to-segment distance. 05 / LIMITATIONS

Read the score carefully

  • Reports are not all incidents. Data can be delayed, missing, reclassified, or affected by reporting patterns.
  • Proximity is approximate. The current implementation compares reports against route points; sparse route points may miss nearby incidents between them.
  • Weights are heuristic. The values and decay rate are manually chosen and have not been calibrated here against measured safety outcomes.
  • One number hides context. The score does not account for lighting, foot traffic, construction, transit access, or conditions at the time of travel.

Use the app as an exploratory prototype, not as a verified safety assessment or emergency service.
